# HMI & Cognitive Display Wireframes Specification
**Feature:** `FEAT-L3-HWY-CHAUFFEUR`  
**Standard:** UN R157, UN R171, ISO 15007  

---

### 1. Cluster Status Icons & Illumination Spectrum
* **L2+ Assisted Cruise (DCAS):** Solid Blue Steering Wheel + Active DMS Eye Tracking Badge. Driver must maintain gaze on road.
* **L3 Automated Highway Pilot (ALKS):** Solid Teal Steering Wheel + Cyan Highway Horizon Glow. Driver permitted off-task activities.
* **Transition of Control (TOR) Stage 1 (Advisory):** Pulsing Amber Wheel (1 Hz) + Advisory Audio Chime ("Please resume steering within 6 seconds").
* **Transition of Control (TOR) Stage 2 (Urgent):** High-Contrast Red Flashing Banner (2 Hz) + Urgent Acoustic Tone (85 dBA) + Active Seatbelt Vibration Tug.
* **Minimum Risk Maneuver (MRM):** Red Hazard Standstill Symbol + Hazard Flashers Enabled + SOS Emergency Dispatch Notification.
