# Legal & Homologation Brief: Autonomous Highway Driving
**Feature:** `FEAT-L3-HWY-CHAUFFEUR`  
**Target Markets:** Germany (StVG § 1a-1f / AFGBV) & European Union (UN R157, UN R171, UN R155/156, EU AI Act)  

---

### 1. Division of Dynamic Driving Task (DDT) Liability
* **L2+ Mode (UN R171 DCAS):** Driver retains legal liability at all times. Driver Monitoring System (DMS) enforces visual attention (eyes-on).
* **L3 Mode (UN R157 ALKS):** Vehicle OEM assumes dynamic driving task liability while system is engaged within its certified ODD. Driver is legally relieved of driving duties until a valid Transition Demand (TOR) is issued.
* **Transition of Control (TOR) Window:** System guarantees at least 10.0 seconds of autonomous fallback trajectory tracking before demanding resumption of manual control.
* **DSSAD / EDR:** Autonomous states, overrides, and handovers are logged to crash-hardened memory for 180 days per UN R157 Paragraph 7.1.
